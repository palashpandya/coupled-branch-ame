5. **Completeness of the as-built coupled result.** Because 1<K<p, the pointwise projective Pauli stabilizer of W is exactly S_p. Indeed a nonzero logical displacement X^u cannot fix a proper nonempty subset of the prime field; a nonconstant logical Z phase cannot be scalar on two distinct basis labels. Thus a prime Clifford preserving W must preserve C_p. Its logical Clifford must be monomial: a nonmonomial single-prime Clifford has all entries of magnitude 1/sqrt(p), and sends a coordinate-subspace projector of rank K to a projector with constant diagonal K/p, not the original 0/1 diagonal. It therefore acts as

    v_a -> ζ_p^(q a^2+r a) v_(t a+d),

up to a common scalar. A binary monomial Clifford has relative phases in {±1,±i}. Their intersection with the odd-prime roots of unity is {1}. With K≥3, matching the phases forces q=r=0. Hence its logical symplectic map must be block diagonal in the recorded logical X/Z basis; Pauli corrections remove its remaining binary signs and supply an arbitrary binary affine offset. The verifier exhausts all binary automorphisms, all prime diagonal logical lifts, and all affine offsets. Singular linear solutions are not confused with Clifford lifts: centralizer preservation and determinant one are imposed explicitly.

Only identity party permutation survives, with the identity and a→K−1−a branch permutations. The kernel factors are S_bin of order 2^(N−log2K), and S_p of order p^(N−1). The extra involution is

    Z_bin^(label_(K−1)) ⊗ [⊗_i Z_i^((K−1)h_i) R_i],  R_i|y>=|−y>, h_i=G[0][i+1].
